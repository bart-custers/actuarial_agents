### Model Assumptions and Limitations

The current model relies heavily on Poisson regression, which assumes that the mean and variance of the claim frequency are equal. However, in real-world scenarios, claim frequencies often exhibit overdispersion, where the variance exceeds the mean. This limitation can lead to underestimation of the true variability in claim frequencies.
