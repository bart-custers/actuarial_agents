- **Policy Tenure**: Calculated the duration of each policy.

- **Interaction Terms**: Created interaction terms between policy type and age to capture potential synergistic effects.

- **Gradient Boosting Machines (GBM)**: Known for handling complex relationships and capturing non-linear patterns.
- **Neural Networks**: Capable of learning intricate patterns but required more computational resources.

Through cross-validation, we found that Gradient Boosting Machines outperformed other models, achieving the highest AUC-ROC score and better generalization across different segments of the data.
