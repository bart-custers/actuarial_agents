2. **Random Forest**: This ensemble method improved upon logistic regression by incorporating multiple decision trees. It showed better performance, especially in handling non-linear relationships and interactions between variables.
3. **Gradient Boosting Machines (GBM)**: GBM further enhanced predictive power by sequentially improving the model through boosting techniques. It achieved the highest accuracy among the models evaluated, with an AUC score of 0.89.

3. **Policyholder Groupings**: Grouping similar policies based on occupation and driving history provided more granular risk assessments.
